Mindful Raccoon

Privacy Policy

Short version: the app collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours anywhere.

Last updated 29 July 2026 · Effective 29 July 2026

Mindful Raccoon is a meditation timer published by Alexey Volvak (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. It is deliberately short, because there is very little to explain.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, transmit, store on our servers, sell, share or monetise any personal information. We operate no server that the app talks to.

Specifically, the app does not ask for and does not have access to:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account credentials — there is no sign-up and no login;
  • your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, calendar or health data;
  • advertising identifiers. There is no advertising in the app, and no tracking as defined by Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework;
  • usage analytics, crash telemetry or behavioural data. We have integrated no analytics, attribution or advertising SDK of any kind.

What the app stores, and where

The app records the practice sessions you complete, together with the settings you choose (session length, bell, daily goal). This includes the start and end time of a session, its target length, whether it was completed, and how it was started.

All of it is written to the app’s own private storage on your device. It never leaves the device unless you deliberately export or share it. We cannot read it, and neither can anyone else.

You are in control of that data

Export. Account → “Export your data” writes a plain CSV file that you can save or send wherever you like.

Delete. Account → “Delete all practice data” erases every recorded session from the device. The action is immediate and cannot be undone.

Uninstall. Deleting the app removes all of its stored data from the device along with it.

Purchases

Mindful Raccoon offers a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks the paid features permanently. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store.

We never see and never receive your payment details, card number, billing address or Apple Account. What the app receives from Apple is a signed confirmation that a purchase exists for this Apple Account; that confirmation is used only to decide whether the paid features are unlocked, and it is kept on the device. Apple’s own handling of your purchase is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

If you use Family Sharing, Apple — not us — determines which family members the purchase applies to.

Notifications, widgets and the Live Activity

The Lock Screen widget and the Live Activity run entirely on your device and show only your own timer and practice figures. They send nothing anywhere. The app plays a bell sound at the end of a session; this needs no permission beyond audio playback and involves no data of any kind.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and is rated accordingly. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. It contains no advertising, no third-party content and no links to user-generated material.

Third parties

The app bundles no third-party SDKs, no trackers and no external content. The only outside party involved at all is Apple, and only for processing an in-app purchase as described above.

Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights of access, correction, deletion and portability over personal data held about you. Since we hold no personal data about you whatsoever, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete — and the data the app keeps on your device is already yours to export or erase at any moment, using the controls described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date. If a future version of the app ever begins to collect anything, that change will be described here and in the App Store listing before it ships, and any optional sync will be opt-in.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or about anything else in the app: alexey.volvak@gmail.com. We answer personally.
