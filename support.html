Getting started

Choose how long you want to sit, press the circle, and put the phone down. A bell rings when the time is up — including while the phone is locked. Pause and stop stay available on the Lock Screen, so you never need to unlock the phone mid-session.

Common questions

The bell didn’t ring at the end

  • Check the volume — the bell plays through the media volume, and the app deliberately does not override a phone set to silent by force.
  • Make sure the session actually finished rather than being stopped early.
  • In Settings inside the app, check that “bell at the end” is switched on.
  • If another app was playing audio, the bell mixes with it rather than interrupting it — turn the other audio down.

How do I add the Lock Screen widget?

Touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customise → tap the area under the clock → find Mindful Raccoon in the list. The widget shows how much of today’s goal is left and starts a session with one tap. The app also has a short illustrated guide in Settings.

What is free and what is paid?

Free, with no time limit: the timer itself, both bells, the Lock Screen widget, the Live Activity, your daily goal, and statistics for the last seven days.

Mindful Raccoon Pro is a single payment — never a subscription — and unlocks your full practice history (30 days, 90 days, the year), streaks and the practice-day calendar, adding sessions by hand, and data export.

I paid, but the app still shows the free version

Open Settings → Account → Restore purchases. This asks Apple for the purchases made with your Apple Account and unlocks the app again. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Restoring is free and can be done as many times as you like, on every device you own.

I have a promo code

On the unlock screen tap “Have a code?”, type the code and tap Apply. If the code is valid the price changes immediately. You can also reach it any time via Settings → Account → Redeem a code.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Settings → Account → Export your data produces a CSV file with every session — start, end, target length, whether it was completed — which you can send to yourself or open in any spreadsheet app.

How do I erase everything?

Settings → Account → Delete all practice data. You will be shown exactly how many sessions are about to be deleted before anything happens. The deletion is immediate and cannot be undone, so export first if you might want the numbers later.

Does it sync between my devices?

Not yet. Today everything lives on the device it was recorded on. Private iCloud sync is planned; when it arrives, your practice will stay in your own iCloud account, which we cannot read.

Does it need the internet?

No. The app works fully offline. A connection is used only by Apple when you make or restore a purchase.

Privacy

The app collects nothing at all: no account, no analytics, no advertising. Your practice data stays on your device. The full statement is on the privacy policy page.
